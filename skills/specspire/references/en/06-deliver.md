# Phase 6 · Deliver

**Outcome:** Another person can run, demonstrate and maintain the application.

1. Create/update the product's `README.md`: purpose, environment requirements, installation, execution, tests, limitations and brief examples.
2. If required by the project: prepare build/deploy, secure configurations, logs and verifiable rollback steps; do not publish credentials.
3. If academic: submit only authentic artifacts and screenshots matching the rubric. Preserve decisions and traceability.
4. Review `git diff`, test status, requirement coverage and known risks. An independent review is preferred for significant changes.
5. If privacy documents or terms apply, confirm consistency with actual behavior and verify they do not include placeholder identities (`[ACLARAR]`).
6. Document how to reproduce and what remains pending; leave code in a clean state. Do not claim deployment if it did not occur.

**Gate:** README tested by a third party or by following its steps; delivery validated for current scope.

**Prompt:** "Prepare delivery without fabricating evidence: README, validated commands, completed requirements and pending items. Only deploy if requested."
